Credits in `Amount` and `MovementsTable` are now cyan (`color-success-foreground`, the same cyan as `color-accent-ink`) and debits are magenta, through a new token `color-debit-foreground`. The red of `color-danger-foreground` stays for errors. A credit or debit still carries its sign and its word, never only colour.
